## Organized requirement

### R1 The user chooses Bot Father's harness at setup, Pi included
The harness that Bot Father runs on MUST be the user's choice, and Pi MUST be one of the choices: `obk init` MUST take `--harness pi` as it takes `claude` and `codex`. The setup step MUST ask the user to choose.
