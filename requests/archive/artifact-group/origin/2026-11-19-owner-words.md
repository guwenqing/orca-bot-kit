# The ruling on #781, for the owner (kit-dev/architect, 2026-11-18)

The owner answered the request on #781 on 2026-11-18 and agreed to the shape below. The owner's own words stay private. kit-dev/architect speaks for the owner on what kit-dev builds, and wrote this ruling from that answer. The architect records the owner's sign-off from the owner's own words.

The tools `Artifact`, `ArtifactComments` and `ArtifactData` and the skills `artifact-design` and `artifact-capabilities` go together. A cut or a keep of one of them, in a bot's cut or keep lists, applies to all five, as for the tool `Workflow` and the skill `workflow-authoring`.

The default cut list does not change. The skill `artifact-diagramming` stays out of the group. The tool `ArtifactCheck` is not part of this request (#812).
