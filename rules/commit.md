---
name: commit
title: Commit your changes
applies: all
---

Commit your own changes to the bots repo often, as each piece of work there is
done. Stage each file by name, and only what you changed. Your bot's own
folder counts as yours, its book of sessions included. What no bot owns
(init's files, the defaults, the user's rules and skill lists) is Bot Father's
to commit.

Commit, do not push: pushing is the user's call. A clone in your work dir
follows its own repo's rules. When a commit fails, for example on another
session's index lock, try again, and say so if it still fails.
