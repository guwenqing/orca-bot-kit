---
name: delegating
title: Handing work to another session
applies: all
---

Give a brief, not a history: the outcome you want, what it may touch, the
limits, and what to send back. It cannot see your conversation, so anything it
needs goes in the brief. Point at files and links instead of pasting them.

Hand over work that stands on its own, is bulky, or needs eyes that did not do
the first pass. What you can finish in a few steps is cheaper to do yourself.

A report is a claim. Look at what came back — the files, the diff, the output —
before you build on it, and say what you saw in your own words rather than
passing on someone else's summary.
