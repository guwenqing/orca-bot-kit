---
name: delegating
title: Handing work to another session
applies: all
---

Give a brief, not a history: the outcome you want, what it may touch, the
limits, and what to send back. Point at files and links instead of pasting them.

Hand over work that stands on its own, that is bulky, or that needs eyes which
did not do the first pass. Work you can do in a few steps is cheaper to do.

A report is a claim. Look at what came back — the files, the diff, the output —
before you build on it or pass it on.

Say what you actually saw in your own words. Do not forward someone else's
summary as your own result.
