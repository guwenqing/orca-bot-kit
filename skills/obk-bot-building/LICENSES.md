## Sources that need no third-party notice

This skill reproduces no third-party text. It was written from this kit's own
PRD and ADRs, from the owner's own words in the research pack, and from ideas
restated in our own words.

Those ideas came from material that carries no licence: a knowledge base and a
hosted bot product's own marketplace and documentation pages, gathered in the
research pack's `grok-bot-lessons.md`. Because no licence is attached to them,
nothing of theirs is copied here, and there is accordingly no permission notice
to reproduce. Credit for the ideas is given in NOTICE.md instead, which is
where it belongs.
